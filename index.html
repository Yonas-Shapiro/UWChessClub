About Us

History

The University of Waterloo has a rich chess-playing history. Since the university’s foundation in 1957, the Chess Club has been “open to all […] who play or wish to learn to play chess”. University yearbooks dating back to 1962 have demonstrated that the Warriors have always had a passion for chess.

Competition

In the decades since, the Club has earned recognition on the national stage. The Club consistently fields a team placing at the top of the Canadian University Chess Championship. The UW team even took home the Crown championship in 2018. The Club also prides itself on its enthusiasm for competition, having fielded five teams of five in 2026: the most of any institution. The UW Chess Club is honoured to be hosting the 2027 iteration of this event from January 15-17, 2027.

Meetings

Within the university, the Chess Club meets weekly. Membership is free and meetings are open to anybody: professors, students, and even non-university community members. The Club welcomes all skill levels from complete beginner to master.

Future

The Chess Club has plans to continue to develop the game across campus and beyond. Chess Club hosts Women’s Socials, First Year Residence Play, and other outreach events on campus. Since Summer 2026, the Club also holds weekly lessons for those hoping to grow their skillset. Collaborations with other student associations help bring the game to more and more students.

Affiliations

Finally, Chess Club is a member of the Waterloo Undergraduate Student Association. With the Chess.com Ambassador Program, the Club is also part of the international network of collegiate chess clubs. The Chess Club is also a proud partner of the Kitchener-Waterloo Chess Club.
