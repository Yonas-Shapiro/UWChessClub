Fall Term Welcome

Welcome to the fall term, Warriors!

Whether you’re a veteran member or new to campus this term, Chess Club is excited to welcome you to the Fall 2026 semester.

Regular meetings will resume on Wednesday, September 16 in MC 2017. These meetings are open to all and will take place every Wednesday from 7:00- 9:30PM.

We’re also pleased to announce that Thursday lessons will be offered again this term, with Novice, Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced sections. Registration will open soon, with further details to come.

Finally, we’re continuing our preparations for hosting CUCC 2027 in January. Team selections for the UW teams will take place in November and December.

We look forward to seeing you all on September 16!
